# Other iOS-on-Android ports: what to take for the S24 Ultra's open hardware areas (2026-11-25)

A research pass (branch `research/ports-hw`, docs only; nothing ported, built or booted). The question: which code
and fixes in other ports would make our open areas work on iOS 27.0.1 (24A446) under xnuhost, and exactly what to
port. Our areas, from the iOSOnAnd tracker (`out/tracker-research/s24.json`, read 2026-11-25): **sensors broken**
(no motion sensors in iOS), **auto-brightness in progress** (the light sensor attaches, no readings), **camera in
progress** (the app opens, the preview is black: CMCapture never starts the stream), **audio works but laggy and
quiet**, **Bluetooth in progress** (turns on, not tested).

Marks: **[F]** read in the named source at the named commit, or in our own phone logs (`out/session-logs/`). **[I]**
this pass's inference. **[U]** unknown until a phone boot or a read of the 24A446 image. Effort is a guess [I]
throughout. "Proof line" is the line a phone boot would print: existing lines are quoted as our code prints them;
lines of code not yet written are marked *(new)*.

Most other ports boot XNU on the bare metal, so their kernel drivers do not transfer. What does transfer: the IOKit
classes, usage pairs and properties iOS's daemons match on, the HID report formats, daemon-side fixes, the capture
plug-in's shape, the audio volume curve, and (for the sensors) a protocol client that Linux on our phone can speak too.

## 1. Sensors (motion: accelerometer, gyroscope, orientation; then compass, pressure, proximity)

### What we have [F]

- Guest: the hosted kernel compiles UniXNU-droidvm's `DroidVMVirtioInput.c`, which already recognises a motion device
  (`INPUT_PROP_ACCELEROMETER` with `ABS_X/Y/Z`, kind 4) and keeps its frame in `droidvm_motion`. But our
  `kernel/patches/oneios-hosted/hid/OneiOSHidInput.cpp` stubs `droidvm_motion_register()` empty ("xnuhost has none")
  and stages neither `DroidVMMotionHid.inc` (the HID services) nor `DroidVMSensorProcessor.cpp` (the SPU user client):
  `grep` finds neither in `tools/oneios-tool/crates/oneios-kernel/src/`.
- Host: xnuhost offers two virtio-input devices (touch and keys; the pen at `/arm-io/input-1`); no motion device. The
  S24 Ultra's accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, pressure, proximity and front light sensor are on the sensor hub
  (SSC), not on `/dev/input` (`docs/hardware.md` 7a, `docs/TODO.md`).

### What the working ports do

- **UniXNU-droidvm (iPadOS 27.0.1 24A446, SM8650 host) [F `droidvm-meta-repo` a18ec72 PROGRESS 10-28, 11-02]:** a
  virtio-input motion device from crosvm (`--input motion`: `ABS_X..ABS_RZ`, gravity 256 per g and angular velocity 16
  per degree per second, iOS's panel frame and signs); the guest publishes three `AppleSPUHIDDevice`s (accelerometer
  `{0xff00, 3}`, gyroscope `{0xff00, 9}` that locationd matches, device orientation `{0x20, 0x8a}` with a vendor
  timestamp child that CoreMotion needs) under the stock `IOHIDEventDriver` ("HID Sensor" personality `{0xff0c, 3}`),
  plus the SPU command channel stub (`AppleSPUHIDDeviceUserClient`, type `ashd`, selectors 0-6) without which
  backboardd and SpringBoard crashed with locationd. Result: locationd/CoreMotion -> backboardd autorotation, and apps'
  `CMMotionManager` read it. This is the only iOS 26/27 proof of motion sensors among the ports.
- **ios-redfin (Pixel 5, SM7250, iOS 16.7.16) [F 23019ab4 / cefcd91 `phone/sensors`]:** `sensd`, a user-space client
  of Qualcomm's SEE (Sensors Execution Environment) on the ADSP: QMI service 400 (`SNS_CLIENT_REQ 0x20`,
  `REPORT_IND 0x21`, `JUMBO_IND 0x22`) over QRTR, protobuf messages (SUID lookup 512/768, attributes 1/128, stream
  config 513, on-change 514, events 1025, proximity 769). `sns.c` (395 lines) and `pb.c` (169) build and parse byte
  buffers only ("transport-agnostic"); `rqrtr.c` is their own QRTR device for XNU, which Linux does not need. Sensors:
  `accel`, `gyro`, `ambient_light`, `proximity`, `mag`, `pressure` by SEE data type; SEE timestamps on the 19.2 MHz
  QTimer. The kernel side (`xnu/patches/0971`, `0972`) posts IOHIDEvents from `SweetHID*` services with the iPhone SPU
  usage pairs (Apple vendor page `0xff00`: accel 3, ALS 4, prox 8, gyro 9, compass 0x0a; pressure on the HID sensor
  page; orientation `0x20/0x8a`). Proximity crash-looped backboardd until it published `DisplayIntegrated`,
  `ProtectedAccess`, `SensorPropertySupported` (0971's message).
- **Jesvi-iOS (Galaxy S20, Exynos, iOS 16.5) [F a13e406 `userland/src/inject_sensors.c`, from Scion's S9 code]:** the
  same usage pairs as *virtual* services made inside backboardd (`IOHIDVirtualServiceClientCreateWithCallbacks`),
  orientation by replacing `CMDeviceOrientationManager`'s methods; proximity needs `Placement = 1`; accelerometer in g
  with Android's sign turned round. Exynos's SSP hub: its data path does not apply to SM8650.
- **iOSPlus10 (OnePlus 10 Pro, SM8450) [F 146e889 `patches/xnu/README-sensors.md`, `docs/sensors-plan.md`]:** the same
  SEE protocol on paper (section C) plus the work a bare-metal port must do first (SLPI boot, registry over FastRPC);
  not proven. On our phone Android has already booted the DSP and loaded the registry, so only section C matters.

## 2. Auto-brightness (the ambient light sensor)

### What we have [F]

- Host: `runtime/bridges/brightness` reads the **rear** sensor `als_rear` (STK6D2x, evdev, clear counts x 1000
  millilux). Every session: `brightness: light sensor on: /dev/input/event7 (als_rear, not grabbed), 1000 millilux per

  the collection's `AppleUSBALSService` (Apple's USB displays). It attaches in a PC boot, but on the phone "AppleUSBALS

  host sends a reading: nothing at open.

  ALS events are made by `AppleEmbeddedHIDEventService::dispatchAmbientLightSensorEvent` subclasses (on an iPhone the
  SPU's `AppleSPUHIDDriver`, usage `0xff00/4`).

## 4. Audio

### 4.1 Lag: merge `origin/fix/audio-late`

[F our logs, `out/session-logs/2026-11-24-mac-gpumem/live.log`] `speaker engine: 3618 periods, taken late by 1622 us
on average (max 98149)`, `engine 5092 periods, 271 late`; the host helper's target rose to 40 ms after 5 underruns
(`oneios-audio: speaker running: ... 5 underruns ... target 40`). `fix/audio-late` (d2f0b46, bf864ec, fd8c487; 2026-11-24,
3 commits, not on main, 84 commits behind it at 2cb113e) makes each engine a guest time-constraint thread (5 ms period, 2.5 ms
computation) and waits with `TIMEOUT_URGENCY_SYS_CRITICAL`; `oneios-audio-rt=0` is the old way; it splits the
Actuator's late periods from the speaker's. Its kernel was built and prelinked (xnu.macho `6d40f8e0`, kernel.kc
`853068df`); not booted. `git merge-tree` against main (2cb113e): conflicts in `docs/switches.md`,
`runtime/xnuhost/src/config.rs`, `runtime/xnuhost/src/config/tests.rs` (the switches line) and
`tools/oneios-tool/tests/kernelgen/golden/staged.sha256` (to be regenerated): all mechanical. Then rebuild the kernel
from the merged tree and boot. No other port has a lag fix for a guest under a host scheduler: the bare-metal ports'
audio timing (redfin's FastRPC/ADSP, sheng's AudioReach) is driver timing we do not have.

Proof lines (the branch's): `DroidVMAudio: Speaker engine: realtime in the guest (period 5 ms, computation 2.5 ms,
constraint 5 ms) ...`; `ONEIOS AUDIO: late periods: speaker N over 1 ms, N over 5 ms, ...` with `over 5 ms` near 0;
`speaker engine: ... taken late by` under 500 us on average; the helper's `target` back at 10-15 ms.

### 4.2 Quiet: the volume curve

[F our logs] `DroidVMAudio: speaker volume 70 of 128 (-29.0 dB, gain 2325 of 65536), change 3: iOS's volume, applied
here (the host plays at full scale)`, also `100 of 128 (-14.0 dB)`, `79 of 128 (-24.5 dB)`; and `oneios-audio: host
volume: One UI's media volume ... set to the maximum for the session`. So the host is at full scale and the guest
attenuates by what iOS asks on our control: -64..0 dB, 0.5 dB a step, linear (`staged/audio.rs`, block
"fix-audio-volume").

[F UniXNU-sheng c8f92b2 `hal/ShengAudioPlugIn.c` 204-210, iPadOS 27.0.1 24A446]: "The audio server maps its user
volume onto this range: a -96 dB floor made a third of full volume -56 dB (i532), a linear -40 dB range still -26 dB
(i533, quiet); now it is -16 dB" with `kMinDb = -36` and `dB = -36 * (1 - scalar)^2`. The same quiet our owner reports,
