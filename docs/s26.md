# The Galaxy S26 Ultra (SM-S948B, m3q, SM8850) port

Status 2026-11-23 (`port-sm8850-s26`): **no S26 Ultra here; nothing below was run on one by this tree.** What this
tree has for the phone came from one fork, by host tests and by its own on-phone records:

- `YMGPwcca/OneiOS` (the runtime; 6 branches, default `feat/oneios-sm8850-m3q`, the newest
  `feat/sm8850-full-bridges-takeover` of 2026-11-22; 453-616 own commits each since 2026-11-15) and
- `YMGPwcca/OneOS` (its app fork; `feat/sm8850-full-bridges-takeover`, 165 commits over our `oneios` and 46 behind it).

Their phone: one SM-S948B, firmware `S948BXXS4BZIG`, One UI 9.0 on Android 17, root by KernelSU, SELinux enforcing.
Their best result: iOS 27 booted to its first frame and ran about 600 s with the 3D GPU, through a root-only
"Framework takeover" experiment of their own (not through the OneOS app's switch, which their app refuses on this
phone). Their own records say the return to One UI in place and the watchdog hand-off are **not** verified; the run
of 2026-11-21 ended in a restart (their thermal guard at an experimental 55 C limit, or another cause: their records
do not say which).

Marks as in [s25.md](s25.md): **[F]** read on a phone or in a source, **[I]** inferred, **[U]** unknown until a phone
shows it. The record of the phone is [profiles/host-galaxy-s26-ultra-sm8850.toml](../profiles/host-galaxy-s26-ultra-sm8850.toml);
what xnuhost does differently on it is the `galaxy-s26-ultra` row of `runtime/bridges/hw/host_device.rs`
([hardware.md](hardware.md) 7h).

## What was taken from `YMGPwcca/OneiOS`, and what was not (2026-11-23)

The earlier passes (port-sm8850, port-sm8850-2, port-metal-haptics: [forks.md](forks.md), the S26 row) took the
input, brightness, display, thermal and haptic work. This pass added the phone's facts and two stand-ins (above).
Not taken, with the reasons:

- the Framework-takeover tooling (`scripts/phone/sm8850-*.py`, `framework-stage.sh`, `recovery-dry-run.py`, the
  watchdog attribution tools, `sm8850_stage_admission.h`, `sm8850-full-bridges` and `cpu-affinity-bench` in
  `config.c`/`main.c`): Python (retired here), phone-gated to their one phone, and the takeover breaks the way-back
  rules (an exclusive touch grab in a session, a restart as the way back) (since 2026-11-23 the grab, without the
  restart, is an opt-in mode: the last section);
- their GPU and display refactor (it deletes our `0076`/`0078`/`0080`/`0081`, `display-direct`, `ram-view`,
  `blk-late-flush`, the Oryon rule's later form, the Wi-Fi `IFF_UP` fix);
- the audio prefill/xrun chain, the encoder binder-pool/EOS changes, the haptic compose stack: general, but each needs
  an S24 Ultra check of its own. **2026-11-28** (`port/fork-backports-2026-11-28`, forks.md): the xrun coalescing
  (`c4d9e318`) taken on every phone, the probe prefill (`7cf70ce7`, `4dd7242c`) behind `ONEIOS_AUDIO_PROBE_PREFILL=1`
  (this row sets it); the binder pool (`72517d38`) was already here (the Rust helper starts libbinder_ndk's and
  libhidlbase's pools on every phone); the helper-error-instead-of-EOS change (`f2570647`) still not taken: our
  device side has no `ENC_MSG_ERROR` path, and the helper's EOS after its 2 s drain wait is what the S24 Ultra's
  sessions have run with (whether a codec there ever needs it is not checked: a phone A/B first);
- `kernel/profiles/iphone18-2-ios27.md`: only their banner and "the earlier reference device" in place of the S24 Ultra;
- no host profile, `verify-host.sh` row or probe exists there (this tree made its own from their measured facts).
