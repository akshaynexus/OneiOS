# Other ports' fixes for the S24 Ultra's GPU, heat and stability (research, 2026-11-25)

What other iOS-on-Android ports have fixed since our pins that would fix the open areas of the Galaxy S24 Ultra
(SM8650, Adreno 750, iOS 27.0.1 24A446 under xnuhost) on the iOSOnAnd tracker: gpu (40-58 fps idle, 20-32 on
launches and Control Center, about 10 in app open and close), metal, thermal (SoC 92-95 C, every cluster at its top
clock), kernel-mem (the unmap storm), stability, ipc. Research only: **no code ported, nothing built or booted.**
Each row says what to port, from where, into which of our files, behind which switch, and the line a phone boot shows.

Marks, as in [references.md](references.md): **[F]** read in the named repo at the named commit; **[I]** this
survey's inference; **[U]** unknown until a phone boot. Every gain figure is the source's own measurement on its own
phone, not ours.

**How it was read.** `gh api` (commit lists, compares, file contents) and blobless clones into
`out/research-repos/` (removed after), read only: none of their scripts or builds ran. The one thing run here:
`git apply --check` of the Turnip patch in section 4 against our staged Mesa tree
(`out/metal/root/.cache/mesa-work`, cecc0e9 plus our patches): it applies (4 hunks, offset 38 lines).

**What was already known** (not repeated): `docs/s24-fps-heat-camera.md` on `fix/s24-fps-heat-camera` (zizhan's
display-link rate, ASTC, swap fence, panel pool, BO pool; ios-garnet's snapshot format; the camera leads), the shader
fixes 0101/0102 of hakios-arm64e-dada (`userland/metal/patches/metal2vulkan`), drm2kgsl's priorities, deadline level
and vCPU uclamp (our `runtime/xnuhost/drm2kgsl/patches/0008-*`, from Droid-VM/virglrenderer), the YumikoHayamato
S25 Ultra items of 2026-11-22 (`docs/forks.md`).

## 1. The two findings that matter most

1. **Droid-VM moved on past every pin we took from it, on our SoC and our iOS build.** On 2026-11-16 their iPadOS 27.0.1
   (24A446) guest went from metal2vulkan 624bab39 (ours) to **555b13d5** (the old runtime, 2026-11-10, before upstream
   deleted it on 2026-11-20) with **NativeMetal 935c468** (153 commits past our b0d6a07) and 21 of their 29 board
   translator patches carried over, 7 already upstream, 0034 dropped; Metal Check 41/42 and the Home Screen on their
   phones [F] (`droidvm-meta-repo` PROGRESS.md, 11-16 entry; Droid-VM/UniXNU `421e982`, `6efbb97`). That stack has
   purgeable-memory discard, single-sample rendering for every client, a system shader cache, video frames sampled in
   place, the render-server encoding thread at CoreAnimation's priority and a GPU-hang fix in Turnip. It is a
   **third road** beside our pin and the rt port (`userland/metal/rt`): a rebase onto a runtime 3,555 commits newer
   than ours but with the API our bridge calls, done once by Droid-VM already. Section 3 row M1.
2. **The display helper's time is cache maintenance, not the copy.** YumikoHayamato measured on the S25 Ultra
   (`cdb297bbf`, 2026-11-23, simpleperf at 1080x2340, 60 fps): `dcache_inval_poc` 36%, `dcache_clean_poc` 31%,
   `arch_sync_dma_for_cpu`/`_for_device` 9% each, the copy itself 10%; `ANativeWindow_lock` is `Surface::lock`, whose
   buffers are SW_READ_OFTEN | SW_WRITE_OFTEN, so every lock and post cleans and invalidates the whole buffer whatever
   the damage. Three write-only AHardwareBuffers on a child SurfaceControl: **114 fps at 17.1% of a core against 60 fps
   at 49.5%** [F]. Our helper locks the window the same way (`runtime/display/src/xhd/run.rs`, `README.md` "the layer's
   buffer is locked whole") on a 1440x3120 frame, 1.8 times their pixels [F]. Section 2 row G1.

## 7. Read and not taken

- **hakios-metal / ios-redfin** (MIT): a native Metal driver for iOS 16 on bare-metal Adrenos (draw culling,
  uncached-read avoidance, render-target reuse, IOSurface use counts 1258, triple-buffered composition 1262-1263,
  wakefix 1264/1294). Every one of them is in their own kernel display or GPU driver, which our path does not have
  (we have QuartzCore, metal2vulkan and Turnip over KGSL). One idea that maps: redfin's 1105 note that CA's swap ring
  is 3 entries and becomes a 2-surface chain when every SwapWait poll answers 0 at once; whether our guest display
  answers SwapWait for three surfaces (Droid-VM's board sets `ca-triple-buffer=1`; no such word is in our boot
  arguments' code) is not checked here [U]. Side find, not GPU: redfin `2f4e857` (1298) reposts the last
  ALS sample within 15 ms when CoreBrightness turns the sensor's report interval on, before its 250 ms
  `FirstALSSampleTimeout`: a lead for our auto-brightness ("no readings come through").
- **hakios-arm64e-dada** (none stated): its newer bridge patches 0201-0203 are a metal2vulkan 0dfdbcd API port and two
  diagnostics; its notes say the UniXNU bridge patches 0009-0015 (which we have) are its missing performance. 0103
  and 0104 we have staged or left out already.
- **UniXNU/AdrenoMetal and Allenwdk/AdrenoMetal** (MIT): since 2026-11-16 on a later development runtime ("R2507")
  with staged row copies, tiling costs, compute command reuse and a chunked system shader cache. A fourth road
  beside M1, unproven on an iPhone; M1 is the one proven on our SoC and build.
- **Droid-VM/NativeAdreno `cdaa0bb`** (20 commits): BO pool, ring, hangcheck and SMMU work of the native sheng
  driver; we use only its virtio-gpu face (references.md 2).
- **Droid-VM/virglrenderer** since 7e17c8e: deadline queues, priorities and the vCPU uclamp are ours already
  (drm2kgsl 0008); `5faa56f` is G5.
- **zizhan's** newer commits (2026-11-21..24): `17ccd80` is T4; `67074634a` closed their app-open stall as the launch
  transition itself (no fix); `9c98beba2`'s misfit migration did not shorten it (off by default there).
- **kerrinz/UniXNU-zorn** (SM8650, iOS 26.6.2): no GPU acceleration yet. **tenku-png/UniXNU-caihong**: archived at the
  shared cache. **alpersamur3/asoft-ios(-android)** (MIT): iOS 26 in QEMU's virtual Mac with Apple's paravirtual GPU
  over Vulkan, not KGSL; nothing for our path. **Aymon-njz/hakios-arm64e-Onyx26**, **oneplus-ace-6t**: GPU in progress,
  nothing published to take.
- **UniXNU/UniXNU-sheng** since 2026-11-05: sleep and wake, Wi-Fi recovery, touch drift, desktop timing captures;
  its `porting.toml` notes iconservicesagent on 24A446 runs with `CI_ENABLE_METAL_GPU=0` under a 15 MiB inactive
  jetsam limit (read, not acted on).

## 8. The order to port them, and who

1. **S1** (Turnip submit split) and **S3** (the camera's reflection crash): small, fixes, no switch. Both edit
   `userland/metal` and `oneios-ios/src/metal.rs`, the s24 branch's files, and S3 is its camera: **give both to the
   `fix/s24-fps-heat-camera` sub-agent** (or port them right after it merges).
2. **G1** (write-only present buffers): the s24 branch's planned "zero-copy panel pool" step 1, measured already.
   **Fits the s24 sub-agent** as that step.
3. **T2** (virtio-media interrupts): the codec archive, nobody's files now: a new port sub-agent. Done on fix/codec-irq
   (2026-11-25), built and host-tested, waiting for a phone boot.
4. **M1** (the plug-in on Droid-VM's 11-16 stack): a new sub-agent, after the s24 branch merges (it edits the same
   build). It brings G4, T3, K2 and S4 at once; decide it against the rt port first.
5. **G3** (the priming list), **T4** (GPU level with the display off), **G5** (the ringbuffer): small, one sub-agent.
6. **T5** (wallpaper freeze): only if the posters still burn the CPU after 0100 names their killer.

Nothing here was booted. Each row's gain is its source's figure on its own phone.
